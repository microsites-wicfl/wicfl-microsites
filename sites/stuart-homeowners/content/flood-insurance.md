## What does flood insurance cover?

Flood insurance is designed to address certain types of direct physical loss caused by flooding, subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions of the policy. Depending on the policy, coverage may include eligible parts of the building and certain personal property.

Covered building components can include the foundation and structural components, electrical and plumbing systems, HVAC equipment, water heaters, and permanently installed fixtures. Certain personal belongings may also be eligible for coverage under a flood policy, subject to policy limits, exclusions and conditions. Not everything inside or outside the home is automatically covered, so reviewing the policy details is important.

## Does homeowners insurance cover flooding?

Generally, standard homeowners insurance does not cover damage caused by flooding. This distinction is especially important for homeowners in areas near rivers, canals, lakes and coastal waters.

Flood insurance can be purchased separately through the National Flood Insurance Program (NFIP) or, when available and appropriate, through private flood insurance providers. Coverage, limits and eligibility vary by policy and insurer.

## What should you consider before buying flood insurance?

Flood insurance is not one-size-fits-all. When evaluating coverage, consider your property's location, flood zones, elevation, drainage and proximity to waterways can all be relevant. Consider your lender's requirements, if your property is in an area where flood insurance is required by your mortgage lender, you may need to maintain coverage as a condition of your loan. Some flood policies have waiting periods before coverage becomes effective. Review what the policy covers, what it excludes, and whether the limits are appropriate for your property.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is flood insurance required in Stuart?**
It depends on factors such as the property's location, flood zone and mortgage requirements. Even when it is not required, homeowners may still choose to purchase coverage based on their individual flood exposure.

**Does flood insurance cover storm surge?**
Flood insurance can cover certain flood damage caused by storm surge, subject to the policy's terms and conditions.

**Can I buy flood insurance if I'm not in a high-risk flood zone?**
Yes. Flooding can occur outside designated high-risk areas, and flood insurance may be available depending on the property and insurance program.

**Is flood insurance included in homeowners insurance?**
Generally, no. Flood insurance is typically a separate policy from standard homeowners insurance.
