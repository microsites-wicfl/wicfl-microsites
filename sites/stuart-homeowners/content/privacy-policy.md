This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit stuarthomeownersinsurance.com, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Stuart Homeowners Insurance is a website operated by Walker Insurance Agency, an independent insurance agency serving Stuart, Florida and Martin County. "We", "us" and "our" refer to Walker Insurance Agency.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** When you request a quote through our form, we ask for the ZIP code and address of the property, the reason for your request, your first and last name, your phone number, your email address, when you need coverage, the name of your current insurer and, optionally, your current annual premium. After you submit the form, you may choose to upload a copy of your current policy declaration page (PDF or image).

**Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, we collect technical information when you visit: the pages you view, the links and buttons you click (including when you click to call us), the approximate region you connect from, your browser and device type, and a cookie or similar identifier used by our analytics service to tell visits apart. We do not use this information to identify you by name.

**Phone calls.** If you call the number shown on this website, we receive your phone number and the date and time of the call. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want your call recorded, you can let us know at the start of the call or contact us by email instead.

## How we use your information

We use the information you give us to prepare and discuss homeowners insurance options for your property, to contact you about your request, to answer your questions, and to keep records of our communications with you as an insurance agency is required to do. We use the automatically collected information to understand how the website is used, to measure which pages and features lead to quote requests and calls, and to improve the site.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

## Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run this website and to handle your request. They may process your information only on our behalf and under their own privacy commitments:

- **Our customer relationship system (GoHighLevel):** stores your quote request and our follow-up with you.
- **Cloudflare:** hosts this website, protects it against abuse, and stores the policy declaration file you upload in a private storage bucket that is not accessible from the public internet.
- **Google (Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager):** measures how the website is used. Google may set cookies in your browser for this purpose. You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
- **Google Maps Platform:** when address suggestions are enabled on the quote form, the address text you type is sent to Google to show you matching addresses.
- **Our phone provider (GoTo):** connects and may record calls to the number on this website.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

## How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and related communications for as long as needed to serve you and to meet the record-keeping requirements that apply to insurance agencies in Florida. Uploaded policy declaration files are kept only as long as needed to review your request. Analytics data is retained according to our analytics provider's settings and is not linked to your name.

## Your choices

- **Analytics cookies.** You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The website still works without them.
- **Marketing calls, texts and emails.** You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time by replying STOP to a text message, using the unsubscribe link in an email, or contacting us using the details below.
- **Access, correction and deletion.** You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to the records we are required to keep. We will respond within a reasonable time.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) across the website, private storage for uploaded files, and access limited to the people who need it to handle your request. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Children

This website is intended for adults seeking homeowners insurance. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the website after a change means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or about how we handle your information, contact Walker Insurance Agency at info@stuarthomeownersinsurance.com or (772) 247-0106.
