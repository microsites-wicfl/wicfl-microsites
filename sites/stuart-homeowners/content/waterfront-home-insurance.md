## Why waterfront homes can require different insurance considerations

Waterfront properties can have additional exposure to wind, flooding and storm-related damage. The home's proximity to open water, construction, elevation and other characteristics may all influence insurance options.

### Location matters

A home along the St. Lucie River, a canal-front property in Palm City or a coastal residence near Hutchinson Island may have different insurance considerations based on its exact location.

### Construction matters

Roof age, building materials, hurricane-resistant features and other construction details can affect how an insurer evaluates a waterfront property.

### Additional property features

Docks, seawalls, pools, detached structures and outdoor living areas may require additional consideration when reviewing the overall insurance program.

## What should waterfront homeowners consider?

### Dwelling and replacement cost

Waterfront homes can include custom construction, premium materials and specialized features. The estimated cost to rebuild should reflect the property as it exists today.

### Wind and hurricane exposure

Florida's coastal location means wind coverage and hurricane deductibles can be important parts of a waterfront home insurance review. Available coverage and deductibles vary by carrier and individual property.

### Flood insurance

Flood damage is generally not covered by a standard homeowners policy. Waterfront homeowners should evaluate whether a separate flood policy makes sense for their property. Learn more about [flood insurance in Stuart](/flood-insurance/).

### Docks, seawalls and other structures

If your property includes a dock, seawall, boat lift, pool or detached structure, ask how those features are treated under the policy. Coverage can vary significantly depending on the carrier and policy language.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do waterfront homes cost more to insure?**
They can. Location, wind exposure, flood risk, construction, replacement cost and other property characteristics can affect insurance availability and pricing.

**Does homeowners insurance cover flooding?**
Standard homeowners insurance generally does not cover flood damage. Separate flood insurance may be available through the NFIP or private insurers, depending on the property and eligibility.

**Does a waterfront home need wind insurance?**
Wind coverage depends on the policy and carrier. Some Florida policies include wind coverage while others may have separate requirements or exclusions. Your policy should be reviewed carefully.

**Are docks and seawalls covered?**
Coverage for docks, seawalls, boat lifts and other waterfront structures depends on the specific policy. These features should be discussed when requesting a quote.
