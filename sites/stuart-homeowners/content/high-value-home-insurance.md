## What is high-value home insurance?

High-value home insurance is designed for properties that may have significantly higher replacement costs, specialized construction or valuable features that go beyond a typical home.

### Properties that may need specialized coverage

Luxury or custom-built homes, waterfront estates, homes with high-end finishes and materials, properties with pools, docks or additional structures, homes with extensive landscaping or outdoor features, and residences containing valuable personal belongings.

The right coverage depends on the property and the policy. A higher home value does not automatically mean the same insurance solution will work for every homeowner.

## Does your Stuart home need specialized coverage?

Your home may benefit from a high-value insurance evaluation if it has characteristics such as:

### High replacement costs

Custom construction, premium materials or unique architectural features can increase the cost of rebuilding.

### Valuable personal property

Fine art, jewelry, collectibles, designer furnishings and other valuable belongings may require careful consideration of coverage limits.

### Specialized features

Pools, guest houses, docks, outdoor kitchens and other structures can add complexity to your insurance needs.

### Waterfront or coastal exposure

Homes near the St. Lucie River, Intracoastal Waterway or Atlantic coast may have additional insurance considerations.

### Higher liability exposure

Properties with pools, boats, staff or frequent guests may require a closer look at liability coverage.

## What should high-value homeowners consider?

Dwelling coverage (is the estimated rebuilding cost appropriate for the home's construction and features), other structures (are guest houses, pools, docks or detached structures properly considered), personal property (do coverage limits reflect valuable belongings inside the home), liability coverage (does your liability coverage fit your property's characteristics and personal situation), deductibles (are your deductibles appropriate for your budget and risk tolerance), and property location (does the property's waterfront or coastal location create additional insurance considerations). Coverage availability, limits and terms vary by carrier and individual circumstances.

## Frequently asked questions

**What qualifies as a high-value home?**
There is no single universal definition. A home's replacement cost, construction, features, location and personal property can all influence whether specialized coverage may be appropriate.

**Is high-value home insurance different from standard homeowners insurance?**
It can be. High-value policies may offer different coverage structures, limits and options designed around more complex or higher-value properties.

**Does a waterfront location affect high-value home insurance?**
It can. Waterfront and coastal properties may have additional considerations related to location, construction, wind exposure and flood risk.

**Are valuable belongings covered?**
Some valuable items may have specific limits under a homeowners policy. Depending on the item and its value, additional coverage or scheduling may be appropriate.
